How this guide works

Each lesson (A–F) opens with the core rules and two diagrams, then two fully worked examples, a short Rules to remember checklist, a physics meme break, and 20 practice exercises. Every lesson's checklist is collected again in the Rules to remember cheat sheet at the bottom of the page. Type an answer and press Check for instant feedback, or open Show solution to see the steps. Numeric answers can be typed with or without units — 2 and 2 s both pass. Throughout this unit take g = 9.8 m/s², the speed of sound in air = 340 m/s and the speed of light in a vacuum = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s, so your answers match the class.
