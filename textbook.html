How the textbook gets used: It sets the scope, the order and the difficulty level for each unit. The workbooks are written fresh from that: our own explanations, diagrams, and practice questions. Devon still needs his actual copy for the book's own exercise sets.

Also worth knowing: Chapters marked with a red letter in the book's contents are the Extension topics (e.g. 2H, 11F-G). Those are the parts a Standard student skips, so they are the ones most worth having a workbook for.
