How this guide works

Each lesson (A–F) opens with the core rules and a diagram, then two fully worked examples, a short Rules to remember checklist, a math meme break, and 20 practice exercises. Every lesson's checklist is collected again in the Rules to remember cheat sheet at the bottom of the page. Type an answer and press Check for instant feedback, or open Show solution to see the steps. Type a squared term as x^2, a square root as sqrt (so √(A/π) is sqrt(A/pi)), and note that the checker ignores round brackets and spaces — so (x+3)(x-2) is read as x+3x-2. Your progress is saved automatically in this browser.

Assessment & the learner profile

This unit is assessed on Criterion A (knowing and understanding), Criterion C (communicating) and Criterion D (applying mathematics in real-life contexts). Criterion C is communication, so a correct number on its own is not a finished answer — organised working, correct mathematical notation, units, and a sentence saying what the answer means are all being marked, especially in Lesson F. The class uses a TI-84 graphing calculator: use it to check a vertex or an intercept you have already found by hand, not to replace the algebra. The learner profile focus is Inquirers — Lesson E asks you to decide which form of a quadratic to use before you start, and to justify the choice.
