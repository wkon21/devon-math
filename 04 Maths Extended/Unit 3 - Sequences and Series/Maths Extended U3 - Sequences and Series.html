How this guide works

Each lesson (A–F) opens with the core rules and a diagram, then two fully worked examples, a short Rules to remember checklist, a math meme break, and 20 practice exercises. Every lesson's checklist is collected again in the Rules to remember cheat sheet at the bottom of the page. Type an answer and press Check for instant feedback, or open Show solution to see the steps. Numbers can be typed as decimals or as fractions — 13/7 and 1.857 are both accepted — and an nth-term rule is typed like 3n+2. The checker ignores spaces and round brackets. Your progress is saved automatically in this browser.

Assessment & the learner profile

This unit is assessed on Criterion A (knowing and understanding), Criterion C (communicating) and Criterion D (applying mathematics in real-life contexts). As on Summative Assessment I, a bare number scores badly: show complete solutions with proper notation — state a₁, d or r and n before you substitute, write the formula out, then substitute, then simplify. Exercises in each lesson escalate the way the paper bands its questions, from Level 1–2 recall through Level 3–4 multi-step work to Level 5–6 and 7–8 problems set in unfamiliar situations. Calculators are allowed — use one for powers such as 3⁸, not to replace the algebra.
