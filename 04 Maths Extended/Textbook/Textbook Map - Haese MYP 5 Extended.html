How the textbook gets used. It sets the scope, the order and the difficulty level for each unit — what an Extended student is expected to handle, and which ideas come before which. The workbooks are written fresh from that: our own explanations, our own diagrams, our own practice questions with our own numbers. Devon still needs his actual copy for the book's own exercise sets.

Also worth knowing: chapters marked with a red letter in the book's contents are the Extension topics — 2H (algebra of sets), 11F–G (Δ < 0, sum and product of roots) and 21I (compound angle formulae). Those are the parts a Standard student skips, so they are the ones most worth having a workbook for.
