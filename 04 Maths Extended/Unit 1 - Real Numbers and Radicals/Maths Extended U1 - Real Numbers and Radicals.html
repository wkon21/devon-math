How this guide works

Each lesson (A–F) opens with the core rules and a diagram, then two fully worked examples, a short Rules to remember checklist, a math meme break, and 20 practice exercises. Every lesson's checklist is collected again in the Rules to remember cheat sheet at the bottom of the page. Type an answer and press Check for instant feedback, or open Show solution to see the steps. For an answer with a square root, type it using sqrt — for example, write 3√5 as 3sqrt5 (the checker also accepts the √ symbol). Your progress is saved automatically in this browser.
